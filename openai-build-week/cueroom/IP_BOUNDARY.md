# Intellectual-property boundary

This OpenAI Build Week directory is a scoped evaluation surface. It demonstrates one public workflow: teacher intake → GPT-5.6 tool use → bounded proposal → human decision → proof receipt.

## Included for evaluation

- the runnable demonstration code in this directory;
- synthetic sample data;
- public setup, testing, and submission documentation;
- a limited demonstration of the StagePort human-approval pattern.

## Not included or licensed

- production StagePort, Py.rouette, CueR, StudioOS, or Global AVC Systems infrastructure;
- proprietary movement-scoring formulas or calibration datasets;
- customer, dancer, child, studio, or client information;
- private schemas, signing systems, governance playbooks, or commercial implementations;
- trademarks, product identities, teaching methods, choreography, curricula, or literary systems;
- rights to train models on non-public source material or remove provenance.

The demo intentionally does not analyze video, infer physical condition, rank dancers, or authorize movement. A human teacher remains the authorizing operator.
